\section{One Step of the Relay}
\label{sec:relay}

When ring $k+1$ saturates, we have controlled the solution through
$T_{k+1}$. The next step extends these bounds across the pinning interval
$[T_{k+1},\tsh_k]$ and the active interval $[\tsh_k,T_k]$. The moment of ring
$k+1$ supplies the initial amplitude of ring $k$. During the active interval,
we confine the material inside ring $k$ and bound the feedback from the outer
quiet rings. At saturation, the same estimates are available for the next
step outward.

Assume (N1)--(N3), and fix parameters satisfying (P0)--(P6), listed in
Appendix~\ref{sec:param-lists}. Section~\ref{sec:param} shows that they can
be chosen in order and give (SA) and (Det). Fix $n\ge2$ and $A_1>0$, write
$\omega=\omega^{(n)}$, and use the rings and clock of
Definitions~\ref{def:datum}--\ref{def:clock}. All ring quantities use physical
time. The reference growth rate is $\sigma=\sigma(0)$, while ring $i$ grows
at rate $A_i\sigma_i$ with $\sigma_i=\sigma(G_i)$.

With $C_0$ from (P4), let $\rhohat_{C_0,G_i}$ be the transient of
Theorem~\ref{thm:active} in ring-$i$ time. For a ring with a positive
assigned amplitude $\ksh_i$, the envelope and transient in physical time are
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-envelope}
\hat a_i(v)=\ksh_i e^{A_i\sigma_i(v-\tsh_i)},\qquad
\rhohat_i(v)=\rhohat_{C_0,G_i}(A_i(v-\tsh_i)),\qquad v\ge\tsh_i.
\end{equation}
In particular, $\rhohat_i(\tsh_i)=C_0$.

\begin{definition}
\label{def:stop}
If the clock is defined up to level $1$, put $\Tend=T_1$. Otherwise let
$k_{\mathrm s}$ be the level $k\in\{1,\dots,n\}$ such that the clock is defined up
to level $k+1$ but not up to level $k$ (for $k=n$ the first condition is void),
and put $\Tend=\tsh_{k_{\mathrm s}}$. We then say that the clock stops at
level $k_{\mathrm s}$.
\end{definition}

The clock is constructed from $n$ downward. If it stops at $k_{\mathrm s}$,
its activation times are assigned for $i\ge k_{\mathrm s}$ and its saturation
times for $i>k_{\mathrm s}$. In particular, the failed level has an assigned
activation time. We use the quiet, active and debris conventions of
Definition~\ref{def:loc}, including quiet at activation and active at
saturation. Any ring farther out than a quiet or active ring is quiet by
Lemma~\ref{lem:clock}(1).

For $t\in[0,\Tend]$, the bootstrap assumptions (BA)$(t)$ are the following
bounds at every $v\in[0,t]$.
\begin{description}[font=\normalfont]
\item[(BA1)] Rings that have not finished their active interval stay in their
prescribed annuli:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:BA-support}
S_j(v)\subset\Nflat_j\qquad\text{if ring $j$ is not debris at $v$}.
\end{equation}
\item[(BA2)] The accumulated driving of each quiet ring stays on the scale
$\varepsilon_j$. For every quiet ring $j<n$, $h_j$ is defined on
$[0,v]$ and
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:BA-quiet}
\maj_j(v)\le2\Abar\varepsilon_j.
\end{equation}
\item[(BA3)] After activation, the perturbation grows no faster than its
unstable envelope, with the initial transient allowed. For every ring $i$
active at $v>\tsh_i$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:BA-active}
\|w_i(v)\|_{H^s}\le2C_2\hat a_i(v)(1+\rhohat_i(v)).
\end{equation}
\end{description}
Here $\varepsilon_j=\mu s_j^5$ is the scale of the mode-$3$ driving from
the next inner ring, and $\Abar$ is the fixed constant bounding the quiet
response at that scale.
By the \emph{improved bounds through $t$} we mean the following estimates
at every $v\in[0,t]$:
\begin{align}
S_j(v)&\subset\NN_j
&&\text{if ring $j$ is not debris at $v$},\label{eq:improved-support}\\
\maj_j(v)&\le\Abar\varepsilon_j
&&\text{if ring $j<n$ is quiet at $v$},\label{eq:improved-quiet}\\
\|w_i(v)\|_{H^s}&\le C_2\hat a_i(v)(1+\rhohat_i(v))
&&\text{if ring $i$ is active or }v=\tsh_i.\label{eq:improved-active}
\end{align}
In \eqref{eq:improved-quiet}, $h_j$ must still be defined on $[0,v]$.
Equation~\eqref{eq:improved-active} also includes every assigned activation
time in $[0,t]$, with $\ksh_i$ defined and positive. The seed or the hand-off
supplies this amplitude and bound before the clock assigns $T_i$. Since
$\NN\subset\operatorname{int}\Nflat$ and the size bounds are positive,
these strict improvements of (BA1)--(BA3) leave room for continuation in
physical time.

\begin{proposition}[One relay step]
\label{prop:relay-step}
Let $0\le t\le\Tend$ and assume (BA)$(t)$. For $1\le k<n$, suppose the clock is
defined up to level $k+1$, with $T_{k+1}\le t$, and that
\eqref{eq:improved-support}--\eqref{eq:improved-active} hold through $T_{k+1}$.
Assume the conclusions in item~(c) for ring $k+1$ through $T_{k+1}$,
and the following bounds on the amplitudes at the completed activations:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-amplitudes}
\tfrac12\kseed\le\ksh_n\le2\kseed,\qquad
\Klo s_i^5\le\ksh_i\le\Kup s_i^5\quad(k<i<n).
\end{equation}
Then the following hold.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)}]
\item The improved bounds extend through $\min(t,\tsh_k)$.
\item If $\tsh_k\le t$, the clock is defined up to level $k$ and the
improved bounds hold through $b=\min(t,T_k)$. At activation,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-startup}
\begin{aligned}
\Klo s_k^5&\le\ksh_k\le\Kup s_k^5,
&\quad \maj_k(\tsh_k)&\le K_1\ksh_k,\\
\|w_k(\tsh_k)\|_{H^{M-1}}&\le K_1 C_Q\ksh_k,
& S_k(\tsh_k)&\subset\NNm_k.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
\item In case (b), retain the initial Duhamel bound in
\eqref{eq:relay-startup}. If $b>\tsh_k$, Theorem~\ref{thm:active} and
Corollary~\ref{cor:active-moment} apply with $G=G_k$, $\kappa=\ksh_k$ and
$\eta_b=\eta_{b,k}$ of \eqref{eq:etab} (with $\eta_{b,1}=0$).
More explicitly, for $\tsh_k\le v\le b$ and $u=A_k(v-\tsh_k)$, besides
\eqref{eq:improved-support} and \eqref{eq:improved-active} for ring $k$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-active}
\begin{aligned}
&\big\|w_k(v)-2\Re\big(c_k(v)e^{\lambda_k u}\phist(G_k)\big)\big\|_{H^s}\\
&\qquad\le C_2\hat a_k(v)
\big[\rhohat_k(v)+\hat a_k(v)+(\ksh_k)^{1/2}+\eta_{b,k}\big],
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-active-moment}
\begin{aligned}
&\big|2\pi\pout_3[w_k(v)]-2\pi\pout_*(G_k)c_k(v)e^{\lambda_k u}\big|\\
&\qquad\le2\pi C_3\hat a_k(v)
\big[\rhohat_k(v)+\hat a_k(v)+(\ksh_k)^{1/2}+\eta_{b,k}\big].
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The inner velocity field satisfies
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-inner-field}
\|\hin_k(v)\|_{C^{M_h}(N')}\le C_0\ksh_k\jap{u}^3
\qquad(\tsh_k\le v\le b).
\end{equation}
All hypotheses of Lemma~\ref{lem:drift} hold with endpoint $b$ and
$\eta=\eta_{b,k}$, and its two smallness conditions are
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-drift-small}
(1+\Tpin)^3e^{-\sigma\Tpin/4}\le\frac1{64},\qquad
\Cls\eta_{b,k}\hat T_k\le\frac1{32}.
\end{equation}
In particular, at $b=T_k$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-saturation}
|c_k(T_k)|e^{\sigma_k\hat T_k}
\in[\tfrac{15}{16}\delta_0,\tfrac{17}{16}\delta_0].
\end{equation}
\end{enumerate}
On the portion of the pinning and active intervals reached by time $t$,
the confinement and moment estimates
\eqref{eq:geometry-confinement}--\eqref{eq:geometry-moment} hold for $D_{k+1}$.

For the seeded ring $k=n$, assume instead that the clock is defined up to
$n$, the improved bounds hold at $0$, and
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-seed}
\tfrac12\kseed\le\ksh_n\le2\kseed,\qquad
S_n(0)\subset\NNm_n,\qquad
\|w_n(0)\|_{H^{M-1}}\le C_0\ksh_n.
\end{equation}
Then \eqref{eq:improved-support}--\eqref{eq:improved-active} hold through
$\min(t,T_n)$, and the conclusions in (c) hold if this interval has positive length, with
$\maj_n(0)=0$ as the initial Duhamel bound.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For the seed with $t=0$, the conclusion is the assumed
\eqref{eq:improved-support}--\eqref{eq:improved-active} at $0$.

\phantomsection\label{par:relay-geometry}
\emph{Confinement.}
At an endpoint $b\le t$ where $\ksh_i\ge\Klo s_i^5$ is known for every
$i<n$ activated strictly before $b$, (BA1) and Lemma~\ref{lem:geometry}
give the support separations
\eqref{eq:geometry-quiet-support}--\eqref{eq:geometry-quiet-inner}, the
field bound \eqref{eq:geometry-field}, and (Loc) in
\eqref{eq:geometry-loc} on $[0,b]$. For $k<n$, the confinement estimate starts
at $T_{k+1}$ with the material inside ring $k$ in
$\overline B_{2r_{k+1}}$. The support of ring $k+1$ is in $\Nflat_{k+1}$ at
saturation, and the material inside it lies in $B_{ar_{k+1}/8}$.
Proposition~\ref{prop:confine} then gives \eqref{eq:geometry-confinement},
with radius $\varrho_k(v)=\big(2+2\Cstar A_{k+1}(v-T_{k+1})\big)r_{k+1}$.
On the pinning interval, $A_{k+1}(v-T_{k+1})\le\Tpin$. During the subsequent
active interval, it is at most $\Tpin+\mu\hat T_k$, where the lower amplitude
bound at activation gives
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-duration}
\hat T_k=\frac{\log(\delta_0/\ksh_k)}{\sigma_k}
\le\frac{\log_+(\delta_0/\Klo)+5\log(1/s_k)}{\sigma'}.
\end{equation}
Thus (B5) keeps this radius below $ar_k/8$ even across the active interval.
Equation~\eqref{eq:geometry-moment} bounds $|q_{k+1}(v)|$ cubically in
$1+A_{k+1}(v-T_{k+1})$. For $k=n$ the inner region is empty.

\phantomsection\label{par:relay-quiet}
\emph{Control of the quiet rings.}
Suppose the support separations and field bound
\eqref{eq:geometry-quiet-support}--\eqref{eq:geometry-field} hold on
$[0,b]$, with $b>0$, and ring $j<n$ is quiet through $b$ with an assigned
activation time. Ring $i<j$ grows more slowly:
Lemma~\ref{lem:detuning} gives
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-growth-gap}
A_j\sigma_j-A_i\sigma_i
=A_j(\sigma_j-\mu^{i-j}\sigma_i)
\ge\frac{A_j\sigb(\mu-1)}{2\mu}.
\end{equation}
In the Duhamel bound for ring $i$, this gap allows us to use the endpoint
value of $\maj_j$. By (M1) of Lemma~\ref{lem:majorant},
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-duhamel-comparison}
A_i\int_0^b e^{A_i\sigma_i(b-v)}\maj_j(v)\dd v
\le \frac{A_i\maj_j(b)}{A_j\sigma_j-A_i\sigma_i}
\le\frac{2\mu}{\sigb(\mu-1)}\maj_j(b).
\end{equation}
Together with the radius gain in Lemma~\ref{lem:transfer-step}, this gives
the product comparison \eqref{eq:O-comparison} of Lemma~\ref{lem:O}.
Its support and field hypotheses are exactly \eqref{eq:O-support}, supplied
by \eqref{eq:geometry-quiet-support}--\eqref{eq:geometry-field}.
The remaining hypotheses \eqref{eq:O-smallness} follow from (BA2), (B7)
and (B9), the last giving $2\Abar\varepsilon_j\le\majst$.
The outer-field estimate \eqref{eq:O-feedback} gives
$\etout_j\le\theta_j\maj_j$, and Lemma~\ref{lem:O} applies Theorem~\ref{thm:quiet} to every
$i\le j$, using the local time $A_iv$. Consequently, for $v\in[0,b]$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:boot-quiet}
S_i(v)\subset\NNm_i,\qquad \|w_i(v)\|_{H^{s_h}}\le C_Q\maj_i(v).
\end{equation}
Appendix~\ref{sec:bootstrap-quiet-control} gives the precise rescaling and
checks for \eqref{eq:O-feedback} and \eqref{eq:boot-quiet}.
These estimates also hold through earlier assigned activation times, by
taking each such time as the endpoint $b$.

\phantomsection\label{par:relay-handoff}
\emph{Hand-off.}
Let $k<n$. Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:geometry} through $\min(t,\tsh_k)$,
using the lower bounds in \eqref{eq:relay-amplitudes}, and then
Lemma~\ref{lem:O} with $j=k$ as just verified. This gives
\eqref{eq:O-feedback} and \eqref{eq:boot-quiet} on that interval.
If $\tsh_k\le t$, \eqref{eq:relay-active-moment} for ring $k+1$ identifies
the leading outgoing moment
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-outgoing}
\qs=2\pi\pout_*(G_{k+1})c_{k+1}(T_{k+1})e^{\lambda_{k+1}\hat T_{k+1}}.
\end{equation}
Lemma~\ref{lem:kicktrue} compares the true incoming field to this moment.
In particular $|\qs|\ge(15/16)2\pi|\pout_*(G_{k+1})|\delta_0$, and
Equation~\eqref{eq:boot-kick} bounds the resulting kick. To apply the lemma,
we use \eqref{eq:geometry-moment} during pinning and the conclusions of
item~(c) already assumed for ring $k+1$ through $T_{k+1}$.
Its earlier quiet-ring bounds are \eqref{eq:O-feedback} and
\eqref{eq:boot-quiet}, and its inner support satisfies
\eqref{eq:geometry-confinement} through saturation. For the seed there is no earlier quiet
interval. The remaining smallness conditions are checked in
Appendix~\ref{sec:bootstrap-handoff-checks}.

The factor $|\beta_k|=|\beta(G_k)|s_k^5/(2\pi)$ in the kick turns the outgoing
moment into an incoming coefficient of size $s_k^5$. More precisely,
Lemma~I.\ref{I-lem:uniform} and Equation~\eqref{eq:boot-kick} give
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-incoming-lower}
|\cin_k|=e^{x_k\Tpin}|\Ain(\Tpin)|
\ge \frac{3c_N}{32\mu}\,
\frac{\varepsilon_k\delta_0 e^{x_k\Tpin}}{|\lamref|(\mu-1)}.
\end{equation}
Lemmas~\ref{lem:quiet-time} and~\ref{lem:theta-quiet} give the
bound on accumulated feedback~\eqref{eq:boot-theta} using only amplitudes at levels
$i>k$. With this bound, Proposition~\ref{prop:relay} bounds the entire
response at $\tsh_k$. The seed-size condition ($\kappa$7) bounds the remainder
$\Xi_{k+1}$ by $\delta_0/(\sigb(\mu-1))$. The resulting inequality is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-startup-duhamel}
\maj_k(\tsh_k)\le 2\Cvel\varepsilon_k e^{x_k\Tpin}
\left[\frac{(4C_2+1)\delta_0}{\sigb(\mu-1)}+C_D(\Tpin)\right]
\le\Abar\varepsilon_k.
\end{equation}
The choice of $K_1$ in Equation~\eqref{eq:K1} therefore gives
$4\maj_k(\tsh_k)\le K_1|\cin_k|$. With these bounds,
Lemma~\ref{lem:handoff} compares the actual coefficient $\ksh_k$
with $|\cin_k|$ within factors $1/2$ and $3/2$. It also gives the support,
initial norm and Duhamel inequalities in \eqref{eq:relay-startup}.
The upper and lower bounds on $\ksh_k$ in \eqref{eq:relay-startup} are
the two sides of \eqref{eq:boot-kick} with these factors included,
as checked in Appendix~\ref{sec:bootstrap-handoff-checks}.

By (B6) and ($\kappa$1), $0<\ksh_k\le\Kup\sbar^5\le\kseed<\delta_0$.
Together with $S_k(\tsh_k)\subset\NNm_k$, this verifies the next clause of
the clock recursion. The active size bound at that time follows directly:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:boot-switch}
\|w_k(\tsh_k)\|_{H^s}\le K_1C_Q\ksh_k\le C_0\ksh_k
<C_2\hat a_k(\tsh_k)(1+\rhohat_k(\tsh_k)).
\end{equation}

\phantomsection\label{par:relay-active}
\emph{Active growth and the inner vorticity.}
Consider an active interval of positive length. For $k<n$ this means
$\tsh_k<t$ after the hand-off above. For the seed it means $t>0$. Put
$b=\min(t,T_k)$ and apply Lemma~\ref{lem:geometry} through $b$,
using the new lower bound in \eqref{eq:relay-startup} if $k<n$.
This gives \eqref{eq:geometry-confinement}--\eqref{eq:geometry-loc}
on their stated time intervals. The local active time
is $u=A_k(v-\tsh_k)$. The circulation field of $\omega_{\ge k+1}$ is
included in $L(G_k)$. By Lemma~\ref{lem:inner-field}, $\hin_k$ is
bounded by $\Cvel\mu s_k^2$ times the cube of the
confinement radius in ring-$k$ units. This gives the polynomial field
bound
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-inner-calculation}
\|\hin_k(v)\|_{C^{M_h}(N')}
\le \Cvel\mu s_k^5\big(2+2\Cstar(\Tpin+\mu u)\big)^3
\le C_0\ksh_k\jap{u}^3
\end{equation}
by (P4) and the lower bound in \eqref{eq:relay-startup}, as in
Equation~\eqref{eq:boot-inner}.
For $k=n$, $\hin_n=0$.

For $k>1$, apply Lemma~\ref{lem:O} with $j=k-1$ through $b$, obtaining
\eqref{eq:O-feedback} and \eqref{eq:boot-quiet} for the outer quiet rings.
Lemma~\ref{lem:Oprime} then bounds their feedback, using (BA3) for
$\tsh_k<v\le b$ and \eqref{eq:boot-switch} at $\tsh_k$ (or
\eqref{eq:relay-seed} for $k=n$). The term $\|w_k(v)\|_{H^s}$ in the
bound for $\etin_{k-1}(v)$ carries the factor $\varepsilon_{k-1}$;
$\|w_{k-1}(v)\|_{H^s}$ in the bound for $\etout_k(v)$ carries
$b_{k-1,k}=\mu^{-1}s_{k-1}$
(Equations~\eqref{eq:transfer-step-inner}--\eqref{eq:transfer-step-outer}).
Their product is $s_{k-1}^6$, giving
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-outer-field}
\|\hout_k(v)\|_{C^{M_h}(N')}
\le \Crelay\frac{s_{k-1}^6}{\sigb(\mu-1)}\hat a_k(v)
=\eta_{b,k}\hat a_k(v).
\end{equation}
For $k=1$, $\hout_1=0$.

We now bound $c_k(v)-c_k(\tsh_k)$ by splitting
$F_k=-\hin_k\cdot\nabla\Phi-\hout_k\cdot\nabla\Phi$ in
Equation~\eqref{eq:running-k}. For $k<n$, the integral containing
$\hin_k$ starts at $\tauK=\Tpin$ after changing from physical time $s$
to $\tauK=A_{k+1}(s-T_{k+1})$. By \eqref{eq:geometry-moment} and
$x_k\ge\sigma/4$, its modulus is at most $e^{x_k\Tpin}|\beta_k|\Cmom$ times
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-moment-tail}
\int_{\Tpin}^{\infty} e^{-x_k\tau}(1+\tau)^3\dd\tau
\le I_3(\sigma/4)(1+\Tpin)^3e^{-\sigma\Tpin/4}.
\end{equation}
The lower bound in \eqref{eq:boot-kick} and (P2) give
$e^{x_k\Tpin}|\beta_k|\Cmom I_3(\sigma/4)\le |\cin_k|\le2\ksh_k$.
Thus the modulus of the integral containing $\hin_k$ is at most
$2\rT(\Tpin)\ksh_k\le\ksh_k/32$ by (P3).
For the integral containing $\hout_k$, Equation~\eqref{eq:relay-outer-field}
gives the bound $\Cls\eta_{b,k}\hat T_k\ksh_k\le\ksh_k/32$ by (B8).
These give the two terms on the right of \eqref{eq:drift-interval};
for the seed, $\hin_n=0$. The smallness conditions
\eqref{eq:relay-drift-small} give (Coef) and the saturation estimate
\eqref{eq:relay-saturation}. The checks for these applications and
for the rescaling are in Appendix~\ref{sec:bootstrap-active-checks}.

Theorem~\ref{thm:active} and Corollary~\ref{cor:active-moment} now give
\eqref{eq:relay-active}--\eqref{eq:relay-active-moment}, as well as
\eqref{eq:improved-support} and \eqref{eq:improved-active} for ring $k$
through $b$. If the interval consists of the activation time alone,
\eqref{eq:improved-support} and \eqref{eq:improved-active}
come from the hand-off or the seed. No positive-length active estimate is
needed in that case.

\phantomsection\label{par:relay-quiet-size}
\emph{Quiet sizes.}
It remains to improve (BA2) on the intervals just reached. At a positive
time $v$ on the pinning interval it suffices to bound $\maj_j(v)$ for $j=k$.
On the active interval after activation take $j=k-1$ if $k>1$. This ring
has an assigned activation time and is quiet through $v$.
Equations~\eqref{eq:geometry-quiet-support}--\eqref{eq:geometry-field},
\eqref{eq:O-feedback} and \eqref{eq:boot-quiet} hold through $v$.
The conclusions in item~(c) for ring $j+1$ have been proved through
$\min(v,T_{j+1})$; its initial Duhamel bound is
\eqref{eq:relay-startup}, or $\maj_n(0)=0$ for the seed.
The bound on accumulated feedback \eqref{eq:boot-theta}
again follows from Lemmas~\ref{lem:quiet-time} and~\ref{lem:theta-quiet}.

By (M1) of Lemma~\ref{lem:majorant} and \eqref{eq:O-feedback},
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-quiet-absorption}
\maj_j(v)\le\maj_j^{\mathrm{in}}(v)+\theta_j A_jv\maj_j(v)
\le\maj_j^{\mathrm{in}}(v)+\tfrac12\maj_j(v),
\end{equation}
where the last inequality is \eqref{eq:boot-theta}.
Proposition~\ref{prop:relay} and ($\kappa$7) then give
\eqref{eq:improved-quiet} for $j$, as at the hand-off in
\eqref{eq:relay-startup-duhamel}. Appendix~\ref{sec:bootstrap-quiet-checks}
checks the endpoint cases. For every $i<j$, \eqref{eq:O-comparison} gives
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:relay-quiet-outer}
\maj_i(v)\le\tfrac34\Abar\varepsilon_i
\prod_{p=i+1}^{j}(K_O\varepsilon_p)
\le\tfrac38\Abar\varepsilon_i,
\end{equation}
since each factor is at most $1/2$ by (B7). This includes the outer rings
whose activation times have yet to be assigned. When $k=1$ is active
there are no quiet rings after activation.

Equation~\eqref{eq:boot-quiet} for quiet rings and Theorem~\ref{thm:active}(a)
for active rings prove \eqref{eq:improved-support} on the new interval;
Theorem~\ref{thm:active}(b) and

\end{proof}

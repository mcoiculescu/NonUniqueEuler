\section{One Step of the Relay}
\label{sec:relay}

When ring $k+1$ saturates, we have controlled the solution through
$T_{k+1}$. The next step extends these bounds across the pinning interval
$[T_{k+1},\tsh_k]$ and the active interval $[\tsh_k,T_k]$. The moment of ring
$k+1$ supplies the initial amplitude of ring $k$. During the active interval,
we confine the material inside ring $k$ and bound the feedback from the outer
quiet rings. At saturation, the same estimates are available for the next
step outward.

Assume (N1)--(N3), and fix parameters satisfying (P0)--(P6), listed in
Appendix~\ref{sec:param-lists}. Section~\ref{sec:param} shows that they can
be chosen in order and give (SA) and (Det). Fix $n\ge2$ and $A_1>0$, write
$\omega=\omega^{(n)}$, and use the rings and clock of
Definitions~\ref{def:datum}--\ref{def:clock}. All ring quantities use physical
time. The reference growth rate is $\sigma=\sigma(0)$, while ring $i$ grows
at rate $A_i\sigma_i$ with $\sigma_i=\sigma(G_i)$.

With $C_0$ from (P4), let $\rhohat_{C_0,G_i}$ be the transient of
Theorem~\ref{thm:active} in ring-$i$ time. For a ring with a positive
assigned amplitude $\ksh_i$, the envelope and transient in physical time are
\[
\hat a_i(v)=\ksh_i e^{A_i\sigma_i(v-\tsh_i)},\qquad
\rhohat_i(v)=\rhohat_{C_0,G_i}(A_i(v-\tsh_i)),\qquad v\ge\tsh_i.
\]
In particular, $\rhohat_i(\tsh_i)=C_0$.

\begin{definition}
\label{def:stop}
If the clock is defined up to level $1$, put $\Tend=T_1$. Otherwise let
$k_{\mathrm s}$ be the level $k\in\{1,\dots,n\}$ such that the clock is defined up
to level $k+1$ but not up to level $k$ (for $k=n$ the first condition is void),
and put $\Tend=\tsh_{k_{\mathrm s}}$. We then say that the clock stops at
level $k_{\mathrm s}$.
\end{definition}

The clock is constructed from $n$ downward. If it stops at $k_{\mathrm s}$,
its activation times are assigned for $i\ge k_{\mathrm s}$ and its saturation
times for $i>k_{\mathrm s}$. In particular, the failed level has an assigned
activation time. We use the quiet, active and debris conventions of
Definition~\ref{def:loc}, including quiet at activation and active at
saturation. Any ring farther out than a quiet or active ring is quiet by
Lemma~\ref{lem:clock}(1).

For $t\in[0,\Tend]$, the bootstrap assumptions (BA)$(t)$ are the following
bounds at every $v\in[0,t]$.
\begin{description}[font=\normalfont]
\item[(BA1)] $S_j(v)\subset\Nflat_j$ for every ring $j$ that is not debris.
\item[(BA2)] For every quiet ring $j<n$, $h_j$ is defined on $[0,v]$ and
$\maj_j(v)\le2\Abar\varepsilon_j$.
\item[(BA3)] For every ring $i$ active at $v>\tsh_i$,
$\|w_i(v)\|_{H^s}\le2C_2\hat a_i(v)(1+\rhohat_i(v))$.
\end{description}
Here $\varepsilon_j=\mu s_j^5$ is the scale of the mode-$3$ driving from
the next inner ring, and $\Abar$ is the fixed constant bounding the quiet
response at that scale.
The improved bounds replace $\Nflat_j$ by $\NN_j$ and remove the factors $2$.
They include the active size bound at activation, where it comes from the
seed or the hand-off. Since $\NN\subset\operatorname{int}\Nflat$ and the
size bounds are positive, the improvements leave room for continuation in
physical time.

\begin{proposition}[One relay step]
\label{prop:relay-step}
Let $0\le t\le\Tend$ and assume (BA)$(t)$. For $1\le k<n$, suppose the clock is
defined up to level $k+1$, with $T_{k+1}\le t$, and that the improved bounds
hold through $T_{k+1}$. Assume the active estimates in item~(c) for ring $k+1$ through $T_{k+1}$,
and the amplitude bounds
\[
\tfrac12\kseed\le\ksh_n\le2\kseed,\qquad
\Klo s_i^5\le\ksh_i\le\Kup s_i^5\quad(k<i<n).
\]
Then the following hold.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)}]
\item The improved bounds extend through $\min(t,\tsh_k)$.
\item If $\tsh_k\le t$, the clock is defined up to level $k$ and the
improved bounds hold through $b=\min(t,T_k)$. At activation,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Klo s_k^5&\le\ksh_k\le\Kup s_k^5,
&\quad \maj_k(\tsh_k)&\le K_1\ksh_k,\\
\|w_k(\tsh_k)\|_{H^{M-1}}&\le K_1 C_Q\ksh_k,
& S_k(\tsh_k)&\subset\NNm_k.
\end{aligned}
\]
\item In case (b), the active estimates include the initial Duhamel bound
there. If $b>\tsh_k$, in local time $u=A_k(v-\tsh_k)$,
Theorem~\ref{thm:active} and Corollary~\ref{cor:active-moment} hold through
$A_k(b-\tsh_k)$ with $G=G_k$, $\kappa=\ksh_k$ and $\eta_b=\eta_{b,k}$
of Equation~\eqref{eq:etab} (with $\eta_{b,1}=0$). The inner field satisfies
$\|\hin_k(v)\|_{C^{M_h}(N')}\le C_0\ksh_k\jap{u}^3$ there.
Lemma~\ref{lem:drift} applies with endpoint $b$ and $\eta=\eta_{b,k}$,
including $(1+\Tpin)^3e^{-\sigma\Tpin/4}\le1/64$ and $\Cls\eta_{b,k}\hat T_k\le1/32$.
In particular, at $b=T_k$,
\[
|c_k(T_k)|e^{\sigma_k\hat T_k}
\in[\tfrac{15}{16}\delta_0,\tfrac{17}{16}\delta_0].
\]
\end{enumerate}
On the portion of the pinning and active intervals reached by time $t$,
the confinement and moment bounds of Lemma~\ref{lem:geometry}(a) hold for
$D_{k+1}$.

For the seeded ring $k=n$, assume instead that the clock is defined up to
$n$, the improved bounds hold at $0$, and
\[
\tfrac12\kseed\le\ksh_n\le2\kseed,\qquad
S_n(0)\subset\NNm_n,\qquad
\|w_n(0)\|_{H^{M-1}}\le C_0\ksh_n.
\]
Then the improved bounds hold through $\min(t,T_n)$, and the active
estimates in (c) hold if this interval has positive length, with
$\maj_n(0)=0$ as the initial Duhamel bound.
\end{proposition}

interval. If $t$ lies within the step, the proof stops at $t$.

\begin{proof}
For the seed with $t=0$, the conclusion is the assumed improved bounds.

\phantomsection\label{par:relay-geometry}
\emph{Confinement.}
At an endpoint $b\le t$ where the lower amplitude bounds are known for
activations strictly before $b$, Lemma~\ref{lem:geometry} gives the support
conditions and (Loc) on $[0,b]$. For $k<n$, the confinement estimate starts
at $T_{k+1}$ with the material inside ring $k$ in
$\overline B_{2r_{k+1}}$. The support of ring $k+1$ is in $\Nflat_{k+1}$ at
saturation, and the material inside it lies in $B_{ar_{k+1}/8}$.
Proposition~\ref{prop:confine} then bounds the radius of $D_{k+1}(v)$ by
\[
\big(2+2\Cstar A_{k+1}(v-T_{k+1})\big)r_{k+1}.
\]
On the pinning interval, $A_{k+1}(v-T_{k+1})\le\Tpin$. During the subsequent
active interval, it is at most $\Tpin+\mu\hat T_k$, where the lower amplitude
bound at activation gives
\[
\hat T_k=\frac{\log(\delta_0/\ksh_k)}{\sigma_k}
\le\frac{\log_+(\delta_0/\Klo)+5\log(1/s_k)}{\sigma'}.
\]
Thus (B5) keeps this radius below $ar_k/8$ even across the active interval.
The moment estimate in Lemma~\ref{lem:geometry}(a) has the corresponding
cubic bound in $1+A_{k+1}(v-T_{k+1})$. For $k=n$ the inner region is empty.

\phantomsection\label{par:relay-quiet}
\emph{Quiet-ring comparison.}
Suppose geometry is available on $[0,b]$ with $b>0$ and ring $j<n$ is quiet
through $b$ with an assigned activation time. Ring $i<j$ grows more slowly:
Lemma~\ref{lem:detuning} gives
\[
A_j\sigma_j-A_i\sigma_i
=A_j(\sigma_j-\mu^{i-j}\sigma_i)
\ge\frac{A_j\sigb(\mu-1)}{2\mu}.
\]
In the Duhamel bound for ring $i$, this gap allows us to use the endpoint
value of $\maj_j$. By (M1) of Lemma~\ref{lem:majorant},
\[
A_i\int_0^b e^{A_i\sigma_i(b-v)}\maj_j(v)\dd v
\le \frac{A_i\maj_j(b)}{A_j\sigma_j-A_i\sigma_i}
\le\frac{2\mu}{\sigb(\mu-1)}\maj_j(b).
\]
Together with the radius gain in Lemma~\ref{lem:transfer-step}, this gives
the product comparison of Lemma~\ref{lem:O}. Its hypotheses hold by
geometry, (BA2), (B7) and (B9), the last giving
$2\Abar\varepsilon_j\le\majst$. That lemma gives
$\etout_j\le\theta_j\maj_j$ and applies Theorem~\ref{thm:quiet} to every
$i\le j$, using the local time $A_iv$. Consequently, for $v\in[0,b]$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:boot-quiet}
S_i(v)\subset\NNm_i,\qquad \|w_i(v)\|_{H^{s_h}}\le C_Q\maj_i(v).
\end{equation}
Appendix~\ref{sec:bootstrap-quiet-control} gives the precise rescaling and
checks for this comparison. This argument also gives the quiet estimates
through earlier assigned activation times, by taking their times as the
endpoint $b$.

\phantomsection\label{par:relay-handoff}
\emph{Hand-off.}
Let $k<n$ and first take geometry and the quiet comparison with $j=k$
through $\min(t,\tsh_k)$. All the earlier amplitudes needed for geometry
are in the hypotheses. If $\tsh_k\le t$, the active estimates of ring $k+1$
give its outgoing moment
\[
\qs=2\pi\pout_*(G_{k+1})c_{k+1}(T_{k+1})e^{\lambda_{k+1}\hat T_{k+1}}.
\]
Lemma~\ref{lem:kicktrue} compares the true incoming field to this moment.
In particular $|\qs|\ge(15/16)2\pi|\pout_*(G_{k+1})|\delta_0$, and
Equation~\eqref{eq:boot-kick} bounds the resulting kick. To apply the lemma,
we have the moment bound during pinning and the completed active estimates
of ring $k+1$. Its earlier quiet estimates follow from the comparison
above, and its confinement through saturation follows from
Lemma~\ref{lem:geometry}(a). For the seed there is no earlier quiet
interval. The remaining smallness conditions are checked in
Appendix~\ref{sec:bootstrap-handoff-checks}.

The factor $|\beta_k|=|\beta(G_k)|s_k^5/(2\pi)$ in the kick turns the outgoing
moment into an incoming coefficient of size $s_k^5$. More precisely,
Lemma~I.\ref{I-lem:uniform} and Equation~\eqref{eq:boot-kick} give
\[
|\cin_k|=e^{x_k\Tpin}|\Ain(\Tpin)|
\ge \frac{3c_N}{32\mu}\,
\frac{\varepsilon_k\delta_0 e^{x_k\Tpin}}{|\lamref|(\mu-1)}.
\]
Lemmas~\ref{lem:quiet-time} and~\ref{lem:theta-quiet} give the
bound on accumulated feedback~\eqref{eq:boot-theta} using only amplitudes at levels
$i>k$. With this bound, Proposition~\ref{prop:relay} bounds the entire
response at $\tsh_k$. The seed-size condition ($\kappa$7) bounds the remainder
$\Xi_{k+1}$ by $\delta_0/(\sigb(\mu-1))$. The resulting inequality is
\[
\maj_k(\tsh_k)\le 2\Cvel\varepsilon_k e^{x_k\Tpin}
\left[\frac{(4C_2+1)\delta_0}{\sigb(\mu-1)}+C_D(\Tpin)\right]
\le\Abar\varepsilon_k.
\]
The choice of $K_1$ in Equation~\eqref{eq:K1} therefore gives
$4\maj_k(\tsh_k)\le K_1|\cin_k|$. With these bounds,
Lemma~\ref{lem:handoff} compares the actual coefficient $\ksh_k$
with $|\cin_k|$ within factors $1/2$ and $3/2$. It also gives the stated
support, initial norm and Duhamel bound. The upper and lower bounds on
$\ksh_k$ are the two sides of the kick estimate with these factors included,
as checked in Appendix~\ref{sec:bootstrap-handoff-checks}.

By (B6) and ($\kappa$1), $0<\ksh_k\le\Kup\sbar^5\le\kseed<\delta_0$.
Together with $S_k(\tsh_k)\subset\NNm_k$, this verifies the next clause of
the clock recursion. The active size bound at that time follows directly:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:boot-switch}
\|w_k(\tsh_k)\|_{H^s}\le K_1C_Q\ksh_k\le C_0\ksh_k
<C_2\hat a_k(\tsh_k)(1+\rhohat_k(\tsh_k)).
\end{equation}

\phantomsection\label{par:relay-active}
\emph{Active growth and the spent inner material.}
Consider an active interval of positive length. For $k<n$ this means
$\tsh_k<t$ after the hand-off above. For the seed it means $t>0$. Put
$b=\min(t,T_k)$ and apply geometry through $b$, using the new lower
amplitude bound if $k<n$. The local active time
is $u=A_k(v-\tsh_k)$. In the field of the spent inner material, the
monopole is included in $L(G_k)$. By Lemma~\ref{lem:inner-field}, the
remaining velocity is bounded by $\Cvel\mu s_k^2$ times the cube of the
confinement radius in ring-$k$ units. This gives the polynomial field
bound
\[
\|\hin_k(v)\|_{C^{M_h}(N')}
\le \Cvel\mu s_k^5\big(2+2\Cstar(\Tpin+\mu u)\big)^3
\le C_0\ksh_k\jap{u}^3
\]
by (P4) and the lower amplitude bound, as in Equation~\eqref{eq:boot-inner}.
For $k=n$, $\hin_n=0$.

For $k>1$, apply the quiet comparison with $j=k-1$ through $b$. The
feedback from these quiet rings is controlled by Lemma~\ref{lem:Oprime},
using (BA3) for $\tsh_k<v\le b$ and the initial size bound at $\tsh_k$. The outward transfer to ring $k-1$ gains the factor
$\varepsilon_{k-1}$. The return field carries the factor
$\mu^{-1}s_{k-1}$. Their product is $s_{k-1}^6$, giving
\[
\|\hout_k(v)\|_{C^{M_h}(N')}
\le \Crelay\frac{s_{k-1}^6}{\sigb(\mu-1)}\hat a_k(v)
=\eta_{b,k}\hat a_k(v).
\]
For $k=1$ there is no outer field.

We now check that $c_k$ remains close to its initial value. For $k<n$,
the inner contribution to Equation~\eqref{eq:running-k} begins at kick
time $\Tpin$. By the moment bound and $x_k\ge\sigma/4$, its integral is
bounded by $e^{x_k\Tpin}|\beta_k|\Cmom$ times
\[
\int_{\Tpin}^{\infty} e^{-x_k\tau}(1+\tau)^3\dd\tau
\le I_3(\sigma/4)(1+\Tpin)^3e^{-\sigma\Tpin/4}.
\]
The kick lower bound and (P2) make
$e^{x_k\Tpin}|\beta_k|\Cmom I_3(\sigma/4)\le |\cin_k|\le2\ksh_k$.
Thus the inner contribution is at most
$2(1+\Tpin)^3e^{-\sigma\Tpin/4}\ksh_k\le\ksh_k/32$ by (P3). The outer contribution is bounded by
$\Cls\eta_{b,k}\hat T_k\ksh_k\le\ksh_k/32$ by (B8).
These are the bounds in Lemma~\ref{lem:drift}, with the inner term omitted
for the seed. They give (Coef) and, at saturation, the stated interval
for $|c_k(T_k)|e^{\sigma_k\hat T_k}$. The checks for these applications and
for the rescaling are in Appendix~\ref{sec:bootstrap-active-checks}.

Theorem~\ref{thm:active} and Corollary~\ref{cor:active-moment} now apply
through $b$. In particular, for $\tsh_k\le v\le b$,
\[
S_k(v)\subset\NN_k,\qquad
\|w_k(v)\|_{H^s}\le C_2\hat a_k(v)(1+\rhohat_k(v)).
\]
If the interval consists of the activation time alone, these two bounds
come from the hand-off or the seed. No positive-length active estimate is
needed in that case.

\phantomsection\label{par:relay-quiet-size}
\emph{Quiet sizes.}
It remains to improve (BA2) on the intervals just reached. At a positive
time $v$ on the pinning interval it suffices to bound $\maj_j(v)$ for $j=k$.
On the active interval after activation take $j=k-1$ if $k>1$. This ring
has an assigned activation time and is quiet through $v$. The geometric
conditions and the quiet comparison hold through $v$. The active estimates
of ring $j+1$ are available through $\min(v,T_{j+1})$, and its activation
Duhamel bound comes from the seed or its hand-off. The bound on accumulated feedback
again follows from Lemmas~\ref{lem:quiet-time} and~\ref{lem:theta-quiet}.

In Proposition~\ref{prop:relay} the total Duhamel bound satisfies
$\maj_j(v)\le\maj_j^{\mathrm{in}}(v)+\theta_j A_jv\maj_j(v)$ by (M1).
This bound makes the last term at most half the left side. The resulting
bound is $\maj_j(v)\le\Abar\varepsilon_j$, by the same relay estimate
and ($\kappa$7) used at hand-off. Appendix~\ref{sec:bootstrap-quiet-checks}
verifies this estimate at each endpoint. For every $i<j$ the product
comparison gives
\[
\maj_i(v)\le\tfrac34\Abar\varepsilon_i
\prod_{p=i+1}^{j}(K_O\varepsilon_p)
\le\tfrac38\Abar\varepsilon_i,
\]
since each factor is at most $1/2$ by (B7). This includes the outer rings
whose activation times have yet to be assigned. When $k=1$ is active
there are no quiet rings after activation.

The quiet support bound \eqref{eq:boot-quiet}, the active support bound and
the improved sizes give all the improved bounds on the new interval.
The improved bounds at the initial time of the seed were assumed.
\end{proof}

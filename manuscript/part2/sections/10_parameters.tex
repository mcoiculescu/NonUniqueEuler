\section{Choice of Parameters}
\label{sec:param}

Proposition~\ref{prop:relay-step} uses one choice of parameters for every
ring and every depth of the construction. We first set the saturation threshold and the
pinning time, then fix the constants in the activation bounds
\eqref{eq:relay-startup} and the active-ring estimates
\eqref{eq:relay-active}--\eqref{eq:relay-inner-field}.
The seed size and radius ratios are made small after those constants are
fixed. The precise requirements (P0)--(P6) are in
Appendix~\ref{sec:param-lists}.

\begin{lemma}
\label{lem:nonempty}
Assume \textup{(N1)}--\textup{(N3)}. The choices (P0)--(P6) can be made in this
order, with all quantities finite and positive (except $\eta_{b,1}=\theta_1=0$),
and each quantity is determined by quantities fixed at earlier steps or earlier in the same step, as in
Table~\ref{tab:param}. None of the choices depends on $n$, $A_1$ or
$\omega^{(n)}$. Finally, if (P0)--(P6) hold, then for every integer $n\ge2$ and
every $A_1>0$ the standing assumptions (SA) hold with these parameters, and
(Det) holds.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
First fix the ring, its spectral data and the regularity orders. The
interval for $\sigma'$ is nonempty because $\sigb>211/2500>9/200$ and
$3\sigma/4\ge3\sigb/4>9/200$. Choose $\delta_0>0$ small so the quadratic
error is a small fraction of the outgoing moment. Choose $\mu-1>0$
small compared with $\delta_0$ so the kick dominates the subsequent
moment drift. The bound for the coefficient drift after pinning contains
a polynomial times a decaying exponential. Choose $\Tpin$ large enough
to make this factor small.

We now fix the constant $K_1$ in the Duhamel bound
$\maj_k(\tsh_k)\le K_1\ksh_k$ of Equation~\eqref{eq:relay-startup}.
Its formula in Definition~\ref{def:K1}
reserves a fixed allowance for the remainder in the transmitted field
estimate. This allows us to choose $K_1$ using the data already fixed.
Choose $C_0$ to satisfy Equation~\eqref{eq:param-C0}, which bounds the initial
perturbation and inner field required by Theorem~\ref{thm:active}.
The constants controlling the remainder, including
$\Crelay(C_0,K_1)$, can then be fixed.

The seed conditions either bound $\kappa$ by a fixed positive threshold or
bound expressions of the form
$C\kappa^\alpha\langle\log(\delta_0/\kappa)\rangle^N$ by fixed positive
constants, with $\alpha>0$ and $N\ge0$. The exponents are positive because
$\mu>1$ and $\sigma'<3\sigma/4$.
These expressions tend to zero as $\kappa\to0$. The active transient also
tends to zero at saturation, uniformly in $G\in[0,\Gone]$: its initial layer
has uniformly bounded length, while the saturation time grows like
$\log(1/\kappa)$ and its decay rate is at least $\sigb-\sigma'>0$.
Thus every condition holds throughout $(0,2\kseed]$ once $\kseed$ is small
enough. In particular the remainder fits the allowance used to choose
$K_1$ at every hand-off.

After the seed is fixed, the radius conditions have the same form,
$C\sbar^\alpha\log^N(1/\sbar)$, except for spectral continuity and the
conditions imposed at all levels. Spectral continuity gives (Det) on the
shrinking interval $0\le G\le\mu\sbar^2$. For the conditions at all levels, the
confinement radius grows at most like $\log(1/s_k)$ and the outer feedback
has a factor $s_{k-1}^6$. Positive powers of $s_k$ dominate these logarithms,
uniformly for $s_k\le\sbar$. Hence one sufficiently small $\sbar$ works at
every level. It also puts the new amplitudes, of size $s_k^5$, below the
seed threshold. The exact inequalities and these uniform estimates are checked
in Appendix~\ref{sec:param-verification}; they also give (SA) and (Det).
No choice uses $n$, $A_1$ or the solution $\omega^{(n)}$.
\end{proof}
